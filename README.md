Customer Churn Prediction

Week 1: Exploratory Data Analysis

Dataset

Source: Telco Customer Churn (Kaggle)
Dataset Size: 7,043 customer records with 21 features
Objective: Analyze customer behavior and identify factors associated with customer churn (Yes/No)

Key Findings

Customer Churn:
Approximately 26.5% of customers in the dataset have churned, while the majority remain active.

Data Quality:
The TotalCharges column contained blank values. These were identified and converted into a suitable numeric format for accurate analysis.

Contract Type:
Customers with month-to-month contracts show a considerably higher churn rate than customers with one-year or two-year contracts.

Tenure:
Customers with shorter tenure, particularly those in their first year, are more likely to churn. The churn rate generally decreases as customer tenure increases.

Analysis Focus

The analysis explored customer tenure, contract types, monthly charges, total charges, internet services, and payment methods to understand the patterns associated with customer churn.

Tools Used

Python | Pandas | NumPy | Matplotlib | Seaborn | Kaggle

Week 1 Outcome

Completed the initial Exploratory Data Analysis, cleaned the dataset, identified important customer churn patterns, and prepared the data for the next stage of the project: Machine Learning-based Churn Prediction.
